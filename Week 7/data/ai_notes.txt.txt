Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, understanding natural language, recognizing images, and making decisions.

AI can be classified into three categories:
1. Narrow AI
2. General AI
3. Super AI

Narrow AI is designed to perform a specific task, such as voice assistants, recommendation systems, or spam email detection. Most AI systems today belong to this category.

General AI refers to machines that can perform any intellectual task that a human can do. This type of AI is still under research.

Super AI is a theoretical concept where machines become more intelligent than humans in every aspect.

Applications of AI include:
- Healthcare
- Finance
- Education
- Autonomous Vehicles
- Robotics
- Cybersecurity
- Smart Assistants
- E-commerce

Some popular AI tools include ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, and Microsoft Copilot.

Advantages of AI:
- Faster decision making
- Automation of repetitive tasks
- Improved accuracy
- 24/7 availability

Disadvantages of AI:
- High development cost
- Job displacement
- Ethical concerns
- Data privacy issues

Artificial Intelligence is transforming industries and is expected to play a major role in future technologies.
